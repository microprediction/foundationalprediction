Toto 2.0

foundation · Datadog · 4M, 22M, 313M, 1B, 2.5B · licence: Apache-2.0 · GitHub · Model card · Paper (arXiv 2605.20119)

Toto 2.0 is Datadog's family of forecasting transformers (arXiv 2605.20119, May 2026) in five sizes from 4M to 2.5B parameters, with a nine-decile head. The headline row is the largest size.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context through the direct forecast path, one variate per series, no covariates, and the GluonTS wrapper's quantile cap off.

The base checkpoints are trained on Datadog observability metrics and synthetic series only, with no public forecasting data, so none of the panels here can have been seen in training. The five sizes test the paper's claim that accuracy improves with scale.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make Toto 2.0 the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
