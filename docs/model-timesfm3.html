TimesFM3

foundation · Google · · licence: timesfm-non-commercial-license-v1.0 · GitHub · Model card

Google's next TimesFM generation; same decoder-only, patched-transformer family as 2.5, scored on the identical protocol so the two checkpoints are directly comparable.

How it was run

Run zero-shot with a fixed 128-length context, same as 2.5. Research/benchmarking use only under the model's license.

Same decoder-only, patched-transformer architecture as TimesFM 2.5. This study scores the 3.0 checkpoint with its native quantile head, under the identical protocol (128-length context, one-step change series) used for every other model here, so a comparison to the 2.5 numbers on the TimesFM page is apples-to-apples.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make TimesFM3 the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
