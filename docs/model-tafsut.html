Tafsut

foundation · Huawei GTS and EURECOM · univariate base (105M) · licence: MIT · Model card

Tafsut univariate-base (weights 12 August 2026) is a 105M-parameter forecaster trained on about 40M synthetic series and then about 30M real series.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context from the tafsut package 0.1.0 at pinned weights revision a17c92c. The card says series overlapping the evaluation benchmarks were excluded; its real data come from GiftEvalPretrain and the Chronos corpus, and FRED and M4 are not named.

Nine native deciles. The tight arm reconstructs them with quantile_dist.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make Tafsut the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
