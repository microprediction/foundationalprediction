TiRex-2

foundation · NX-AI · 38.4M (univariate) · licence: Apache-2.0 · GitHub · Model card · Paper (arXiv 2607.01204)

TiRex-2 is NX-AI's successor to TiRex (arXiv 2607.01204, NeurIPS 2026 oral), an xLSTM forecaster with a nine-decile head; package 0.5.0 of 9 November 2026.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context, with both test-time augmentations (sign flip, trend differencing) off.

Pretrained on the Chronos datasets and LOTSA, which include M4 and the Monash archive's fred_md. NX-AI also publishes a GIFT-Eval-decontaminated checkpoint, not used here.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make TiRex-2 the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
