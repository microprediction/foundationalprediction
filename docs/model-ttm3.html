TTM-R3

foundation · IBM Research · 1.48M (90-30 branch) · licence: Apache-2.0 · GitHub · Model card

TTM-R3 is IBM's third release of the TinyTimeMixer family (Granite time series), small MLP-mixer forecasters with a nine-decile head.

How it was run

TTM checkpoints are tied to a context length and none takes 128, so the arm uses the 90-30 branch on the last 90 values of the 128-length context. Univariate, no exogenous inputs, no fine-tuning.

Trained on selected GIFT-Eval pretraining and training datasets; the list is not published, so overlap with the M4 hourly panel cannot be ruled out.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make TTM-R3 the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
