Chronos

foundation · Amazon · · licence: Apache-2.0 · GitHub · Model card · Paper (arXiv 2403.07815)

Chronos tokenizes a series into a fixed vocabulary and forecasts it autoregressively, like a language model over quantized values.

How it was run

Reused from the earlier foundation study; run zero-shot on the one-step change series.

Chronos treats forecasting as language modelling. It scales and quantises the series into a fixed token vocabulary, trains a T5-style transformer on the token sequences with a cross-entropy loss, and forecasts by sampling tokens. This study runs the Chronos-Bolt variant (chronos-bolt-small), which patches the context and emits quantiles directly in a single forward pass rather than sampling autoregressively; it is far faster, but its log-likelihood is a quantile reconstruction, so read its CRPS as the primary signal.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make Chronos the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
