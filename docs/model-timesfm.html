TimesFM

foundation · Google · 2.5 · licence: Apache-2.0 · GitHub · Paper (arXiv 2310.10688)

TimesFM is a decoder-only, patched time-series transformer; we score version 2.5.

How it was run

Run zero-shot with a fixed 128-length context.

TimesFM is a decoder-only transformer in the style of a language model. It splits the context into fixed-length patches, treats each patch as a token, and is pretrained on a large corpus of real and synthetic series. This study scores version 2.5 (200M parameters) with its continuous quantile head, which emits the predictive spread directly.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make TimesFM the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
