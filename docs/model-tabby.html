Tabby

foundation · Huawei Paris Noah's Ark Lab · Pretrain (~146M) · licence: CC BY-NC 4.0 · Model card · Paper · Code

Tabby-Pretrain (report 12 September 2026) is an encoder-only patch transformer with an 8,192-position window and 99 monotone quantile levels. The prompt-tuned Tabby is a separate model and is not scored here.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU in float32 with a fixed 128-length context through the official predictor (trustworthyAI commit 8675af0) at pinned weights revision b26c277. Weights are non-commercial.

Encoder-only patch transformer, 99 native quantile levels.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make Tabby the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%.
