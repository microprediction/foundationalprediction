AutoARIMA

classical · Nixtla (statsforecast) · statsforecast · licence: Apache-2.0 · Code

Automatic ARIMA order selection by AICc, the classical workhorse.

How it was run

Refit at every step on the full history to date, so it is online in the sense every arm here is: it sees every observation before forecasting the next. The +id, +aci and +pid arms add a conformal interval, adaptive conformal inference and a PID-controlled conformal interval on top.

Gaussian predictive from the fitted ARIMA; the conformal wrappers adjust its quantiles.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make AutoARIMA the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
