TimeH2O

foundation · H2O.ai · v1 (108.8M) and v1-tiny (5.8M) · licence: H2O Model Non-Commercial Licence v1.0 · Model card · Package (h2ofm)

TimeH2O is H2O.ai's time-series foundation model, released 23 October 2026 in a 108.8M and a 5.8M size, trained on the nine deciles.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context through h2ofm's forecast_many, with the nonnegativity clip and integer snapping off and the package's own patch ensemble and flip-equivariant blend kept. The weights are gated and licensed for testing, evaluation and research only.

The architecture is described on the model card. Two sizes are run here; the tight arm reconstructs the nine deciles with quantile_dist, the original study's reconstruction. Pretraining contamination is unchecked.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make TimeH2O the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
