Moirai 2.0

foundation · Salesforce · small (11.4M) · licence: CC BY-NC 4.0 · GitHub (uni2ts) · Model card · Paper (arXiv 2511.11698)

Moirai 2.0 is Salesforce's decoder-only successor to Moirai (arXiv 2511.11698) with a nine-decile head. Only the small size is public.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context, univariate. Its deciles are not constrained to be monotone; crossings are rearranged by sorting. The weights are licensed for research use only.

Pretraining includes GIFT-Eval's pretraining set, which contains fred_md (107 FRED-MD monthly series), and GIFT-Eval's training split, which covers every M4 frequency, so its monthly and hourly rows measure recall as well as forecasting.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make Moirai 2.0 the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
