classical · Nixtla (statsforecast) · statsforecast · licence: Apache-2.0 · Code

Automatic exponential smoothing with error, trend and seasonal components chosen by AICc.

How it was run

Refit at every step on the full history to date.

Gaussian predictive from the fitted state-space form.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make AutoETS the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
