GARCH-t

classical · Kevin Sheppard (arch) · arch · licence: NCSA · Code

GARCH(1,1) with Student-t innovations: the standard model for returns with volatility clustering.

How it was run

Refit at every step. On price and return series it is the recommendation, and laplace ties it there.

Conditional-variance recursion with heavy-tailed innovations; a fitted scale, not a mean model.

  • Code — https://github.com/bashtage/arch

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make GARCH-t the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
