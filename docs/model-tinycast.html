TinyCast

foundation · RAWS Labs · 146,505 parameters · licence: Apache-2.0 · GitHub · Model card · Paper (arXiv 2608.15767)

TinyCast is a 146,505-parameter attention-free forecaster with a periodogram phase prior and a nine-decile head (arXiv 2608.15767, August 2026).

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context, which the package left-pads to its 2048-step input with the first value; no downsampling, no frequency hint, sign-flip averaging off in the headline arm and on in TinyCast(flip), the setting behind its published GIFT-Eval numbers.

An attention-free model: a dilated causal convolution encoder, a periodogram phase prior with no parameters, and a nine-decile decoder rolled out in 48-step blocks. Trained on GIFT-Eval's pretraining set, with the datasets overlapping the GIFT-Eval and Chronos zero-shot test sets removed (the exclusion list is not published), plus Chronos KernelSynth and synthetic series.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make TinyCast the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
