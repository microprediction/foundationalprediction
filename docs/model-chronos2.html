Chronos-2

foundation · Amazon · 120M · licence: Apache-2.0 · GitHub · Model card · Report (arXiv 2510.15821)

Chronos-2 is Amazon's encoder-only successor to Chronos-Bolt (arXiv 2510.15821, October 2025): 120M parameters with group attention and 21 native quantile levels.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context, one series per group with no covariates and cross_learning off, so a forecast does not depend on the rest of the batch. Its quantile levels are not constrained to be monotone; crossings are rearranged by sorting.

Pretraining includes all 414 M4 hourly series, the whole hourly panel here, and other M4 frequencies, so its hourly row measures recall as well as forecasting. No FRED dataset is named in its training data.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make Chronos-2 the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
