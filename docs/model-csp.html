Conformal Seasonal Pools

conformal · Valeriy Manokhin (csp-forecaster) · 0.2.0 · licence: MIT · Paper · Code · Review

CSP forecasts a series by drawing from its own past at the same phase of the cycle, around a seasonal-naive forecast. Nothing is trained; the pool is the predictive.

How it was run

Run from the author's package as its README directs, default configuration, refit at every step on the trailing 750 observations with 256 samples; season 24 on hourly, 5 on daily, 52 on weekly, 12 on monthly. The combination arms join it with laplace: &lap is the likelihood-weighted portfolio, +lap stacks CSP on laplace's residuals, ~lap the reverse, @lap recalibrates CSP's PIT against laplace.

A same-phase pool and a residual pool around the seasonal naive, mixed and sampled. Its density is conditional on the phase for free, which is where it beats laplace on strongly seasonal series; on series that do not repeat, the pool describes a regime that has moved on.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make Conformal Seasonal Pools the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
