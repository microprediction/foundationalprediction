ci-proto

foundation · Continuous Intelligence · 167M · licence: ci-proto Code and Model Licence v1.0 · Model card · Code

ci-proto is Continuous Intelligence's nine-decile forecaster (code and weights released 11 November 2026): 167M parameters adapted from TiRex-2 and xLSTM.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context through the continuousintelligence package at a pinned weights revision. The licence is Apache-2.0 plus a public attribution notice and a one-time e-mail to the vendor when a product, service or model using its code, weights or outputs is distributed. Training data is undisclosed beyond 'a large corpus of real-world and synthetic time series'; its fev-bench submission declares no fev-bench data.

Nine native deciles, xLSTM backbone adapted from TiRex-2. The tight arm reconstructs the deciles with quantile_dist, the original study's reconstruction.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make ci-proto the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
