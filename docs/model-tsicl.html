TS-ICL

foundation · EDF · tsicl-v1 · licence: Non-commercial · Model card

TS-ICL (EDF, June 2026) is an in-context forecaster trained to emit 99 quantile levels.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context from the tsicl package at pinned weights revision 19c9403. Its public quantile-level check cannot represent some levels (0.07), so the adapter takes every trained level; its quantiles can cross, so they are sorted before scoring.

99 trained quantile levels, monotone after sorting.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make TS-ICL the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
