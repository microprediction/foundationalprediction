t0-gamma

foundation · The Forecasting Company · 256M · licence: Apache-2.0 · GitHub · Model card · Paper (arXiv 2609.24559) · Release post

t0-gamma is The Forecasting Company's third public t0 checkpoint, released 13 October 2026: a 256M-parameter decoder-style patch transformer that predicts 21 quantile levels natively and ranks first among single zero-shot models on fev-bench and TIME.

How it was run

Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context (four 32-point patches) through the direct predict path, with the nonnegativity clip, integer snapping and sign-flip averaging switched off and no cross-series inference. The headline row is all 21 native levels; the nine-decile subset and the vendor's sign-flip averaging are tabled below.

t0 is a decoder-style patch transformer built for forecasting with covariates and multivariate context, with a 32-step patch and a 1,024-step native horizon; none of the covariate machinery is used here. The paper (arXiv 2609.24559) documents the alpha and beta checkpoints; gamma's training recipe is described only in the release post. On its native 21 levels it is within 0.1 nats of Laplace on daily econ, level on price and weekly, and ahead on monthly and M4-hourly, with coverage 0.89 to 0.91. The vendor's sign-flip averaging adds about 0.02 nats at twice the runtime. Pretraining contamination is unchecked.

Against laplace, one step ahead

On the interactive leaderboard you can make t0-gamma the reference and see every other model paired against it, on any panel or subset of series; the tables below keep laplace as the reference.

Per series, Diebold–Mariano on the log score; a win is the model ahead beyond 1.96 standard errors, a loss laplace ahead, the rest draws. Median ΔLL is the model's log score minus laplace's, in nats per step; CRPS ratio is model over laplace (below 1 favours the model); coverage is the share of outcomes inside the central 90%. The methodology page has the protocol; the leaderboards place the model among the rest.
